📍 San Feliciano · ~15 min by car
Faliero - Torta de la Maria
Umbrian icon

A legendary institution since 1969 - practically a pilgrimage for anyone in Umbria. Maria's torta al testo (a flatbread cooked over an open fire and stuffed with sausage and greens, or prosciutto and pecorino) is the real star. Also excellent: fried lake fish, grilled Chianina, homemade pasta. Big communal wooden tables, chaotic at peak times, utterly worth it. Run today by Maria and her sons Luca and Cristiano.

Explore the area

You are in the heart of Umbria, with Tuscany just minutes away. There is so much to discover - here is where we would start.

01
La Rocca di Passignano

The medieval fortress above the village offers breathtaking panoramic views over the lake. Wander the stone streets to reach it - the journey is half the pleasure.

5 min walk
02
Lago Trasimeno by bike

The lake has a beautiful cycling path around its shores. Hire a bike in the village and follow the water at your own pace - the light in the late afternoon is magical.

Starts at the port
03
Isola Maggiore ferry

A short ferry from Passignano port takes you to the largest inhabited island on the lake. Visit the Guglielmi castle, the lace-making workshops and have lunch by the water at Da Sauro.

Ferry from the port
04
Monte del Lago & Castel Rigone

Two enchanting hilltop hamlets on the northern shore of the lake. Monte del Lago is tiny and perfectly preserved. Castel Rigone sits high above the water with sweeping views - drive up in the late afternoon light.

~15-20 min by car
05
Castiglione del Lago

The most beautiful town on the lake - a promontory jutting into the water with a magnificent fortress and lovely views from all sides. Great for a morning stroll, local shops, and aperitivo at sunset.

~20 min by car
06
Campo del Sole, Tuoro

An open-air sculpture park by the lake in nearby Tuoro - a hidden gem. 27 artist columns arranged in a spiral on the lakeshore. Peaceful and utterly unique.

~10 min by car
07
Città della Pieve

A beautiful hilltop town in terracotta brick - birthplace of Perugino, one of the great Renaissance painters. Gorgeous medieval streets and wonderful views over the Val di Chiana. Don't miss lunch at Coppetta.

~25 min by car
08
Perugia

The magnificent Umbrian capital - the Palazzo dei Priori, the National Gallery of Umbria. Two unmissable food stops: L'Antica Latteria for their legendary maritozzo alla panna, and Gelateria Perusia for outstanding artisan gelato. A full day well spent.

~30 min by car
09
Rasiglia

A magical little village built around natural springs and watercourses that run right through the streets. Medieval stone houses, waterwheels and crystal-clear streams - one of Umbria's best-kept secrets. Go on a weekday to enjoy the tranquillity.

~45 min by car
10
Spello

One of Italy's most floral hilltop towns - stone houses draped in geraniums, beautiful Pintoricchio frescoes in Santa Maria Maggiore, and a wonderful atmosphere. Very close to Assisi.

~40 min by car
11
Assisi

The birthplace of St. Francis and one of Italy's most beautiful hilltop towns. The Basilica di San Francesco with its Giotto frescoes is unmissable.

~45 min by car
12
Gubbio

One of the best-preserved medieval towns in Italy - dramatic, austere and strikingly beautiful. The Palazzo dei Consoli dominates the valley. Take the open-air funicular up the mountain for incredible views.

~1 hr by car
13
Civita di Bagnoregio

The "Dying City" - a medieval village perched on a crumbling tufa rock, reachable only by a long pedestrian bridge. Utterly extraordinary and unlike anything else in Italy. Go early in the morning to beat the crowds.

~1 hr 15 min by car
🌻 Into Tuscany

The Tuscan border is just 20 minutes away - these are among the most beautiful places in all of Italy.

14
Cortona

An ancient Etruscan hill town with sweeping views over the Val di Chiana. One of Tuscany's most atmospheric towns - lovely for a morning stroll, great food, and a long lunch.

~25 min by car
15
Montepulciano

One of Tuscany's greatest hilltop towns - stunning Renaissance architecture, remarkable wine (Vino Nobile di Montepulciano), and a dramatic main street climbing to the top. A full morning beautifully spent.

~45 min by car
16
Pienza

The "ideal Renaissance city" - a tiny, perfect hilltop town built by Pope Pius II. UNESCO-listed, breathtakingly beautiful, and famous for its Pecorino di Pienza cheese. Walk the ramparts for views over the Val d'Orcia.

~50 min by car
17
Bagno Vignoni

One of Italy's most unique villages - its central piazza is a Renaissance thermal pool. Ancient, atmospheric, and utterly unlike anywhere else. A perfect detour on the way to or from Pienza.

~55 min by car
